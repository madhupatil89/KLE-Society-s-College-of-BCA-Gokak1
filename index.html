"To transform aspiring students from North Karnataka into globally competitive software professionals with high ethical integrity."

KLE Society’s College of BCA, Gokak was established under the visionary auspices of the Karnatak Lingayat Education (KLE) Society, Belagavi, which was founded in 1916 by seven selfless visionary teachers revered as the 'Saptarshis'.

Affiliated with Rani Channamma University, Belagavi, our Gokak campus brings world-class IT education, state-of-the-art computer laboratories, and professional placement mentorship right to the heart of the region, ensuring every student has direct access to high-growth tech careers.

NEP 2020 Multi-Exit Honors Program

Flexible undergraduate structure offering Certificate (1 yr), Diploma (2 yrs), Degree (3 yrs), and Honors with Research (4 yrs).

Practical Coding Immersion

Dual-boot Linux/Windows labs, Git version control workflows, live software projects, and regular hackathons.

Comprehensive Placement Cell

Continuous aptitude training, communication bootcamps, and direct on-campus interviews with Fortune 500 tech firms.
